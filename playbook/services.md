## The three verbs

Each level is a different thing the client wants, not more of the same thing.

- **Maintain — hold.** Stop what you have decaying.
- **Grow — expand.** Close the gaps, show up for questions you currently miss.
- **Lead — compete.** Be named *instead of* the competitors named ahead of you.

A client can pick from the page without asking us anything, which is the point.

## How the money is meant to work

The audit is the smallest thing sold and the **qualifier for the Foundation**.
Foundations are year-one income — one is over five months of a Maintain client's
revenue, delivered in one go. The monthly plans are what make the business worth
owning later. **So converting audits into Foundations matters more in year one
than converting them into monthlies.**

## Standing rules

- **No free audits, no introductory rate, no "first five clients" discount, no
  bundling the audit with a monthly plan.** A free audit is an introductory rate
  wearing a different hat. Settled 2026-07-31.
- **Tiers separate on permanent answer pages, not question volume.** Separating
  on volume made every step up earn *less* per hour than the one below.
- **No minimum term on any plan**, so a later price rise on existing clients is a
  churn event. Launch prices are the only ones that can be set for free.
- **Not VAT registered**, and every page says so — "the prices shown are the
  prices you pay". That claim is published, crawled and cached, so crossing the
  threshold is a copy change across the whole site.

## An answer page

The unit the Foundation and the upper tiers are priced on. A permanent page on
the client's own site that answers one real customer question properly — the
kind an assistant can quote. We write and publish it; the client owns it.

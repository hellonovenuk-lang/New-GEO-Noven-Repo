# The business

*What Wardith is and where it stands. Start here.*

## What we sell

Businesses used to be found through search. Increasingly their customers ask an
AI assistant — ChatGPT, Google, Copilot, Perplexity — "who's a good plumber near
me?" and act on the answer. If the assistant has never heard of a business, that
business quietly loses the job. **Wardith makes a business visible, accurate and
recommendable to those assistants.** Prices and scope: `services.md`.

**Who runs it:** Kieran Smith, sole trader, trading as Wardith. Based in the
Wirral, working remotely across the UK. One person, no employees.

## The honest position

One person, ten published pages, one product proven on itself, three cold emails
sent, no revenue. **The audit is the only thing that has been done end to end**,
and even that was on our own business rather than a client's. Everything else is
written down and untested.

**The binding constraint is not delivery capacity. It is the prospect list.**
About 18 hours a week are available for delivery, which is six audits — far more
than the reply rate will produce. Getting in front of people is the whole job.

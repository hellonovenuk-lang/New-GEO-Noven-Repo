# Wardith / Signal

Second review concept. The only inherited visual constraint is the supplied Wardith logo. Business facts and published records remain authoritative.

The direction combines graphite, acid chartreuse, Manrope display typography, DM Sans body type, original optical artwork, generous spacing and a short commercial journey. The site imports `signal.css` directly; neither previous stylesheet is loaded.

Visitors move from a clear visibility proposition to an interactive report outline, real historical evidence, and an explicit £250 first purchase. Further services stay optional. The contact action opens the visitor's email app; it does not send anything or enable the gated payment route.

Design references consulted: [Instrument](https://www.instrument.com/), [Linear](https://linear.app/) and [Awwwards agency collection](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/design-agencies/). These informed the attention to art direction, hierarchy and product explanation; no layouts, copy, logos or visual assets were copied.

`public/signal-lens.webp` is original generated artwork: a physical optical lens isolating a chartreuse object among graphite objects. It is a visual metaphor, not a photograph of a Wardith product or a representation of measured results. Optimised to approximately 70 KB; its original aspect ratio is 3:2.

Implementation: existing static Astro architecture, no new dependencies. Tabs support arrows, Home/End and click; mobile navigation supports Escape and outside click; essential report content remains readable without JavaScript; motion honours reduced-motion preferences.

Review only. Keep `main` and the first concept unchanged. The successful production build and static checks do not establish conversion uplift or substitute for browser visual testing.
